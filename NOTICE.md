# Prompt Master: OpenAI edition

Derived from [nidhinjs/prompt-master](https://github.com/nidhinjs/prompt-master), version 1.8.2, commit `49b3a72469b1e7287d66387004ccfcca90e5968a` dated 22 October 2026. The original MIT licence is retained in LICENSE.

Research and modification date: 26 October 2026. Local edition: 1.8.2-openai.2.

Created by alban260112 using AI, including the research synthesis, adaptation, documentation and model-based checks. Based on the original Prompt Master by nidhinjs, whose MIT licence and attribution are retained. This describes authorship of the adaptation, not authorship of the original project or independent human verification of every AI-produced change.

Edition 1.8.2-openai.2 adds proactive prompt improvement for materially unclear action requests. Clear requests, ordinary questions and minor typos remain outside that automatic trigger. The original twelve-case comparison concerned edition 1.8.2-openai.1; the new trigger needs its own checks and should not inherit a benchmark claim from that comparison.

The original nine-dimension intent extraction, tool routing, diagnostic categories, memory block, prompt decompiler and templates A to N remain the base. OpenAI-specific reference files and evaluation guidance were added. Shared rules and several templates were revised where they affected OpenAI use. Non-OpenAI profiles were retained and are not independently validated by this research.

This is an unofficial local derivative. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the original author or OpenAI. Its performance has not been established by downstream comparative benchmarks. See references/evidence.md and the accompanying research report for the evidence and validation limits.
