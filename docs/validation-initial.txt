PROMPT MASTER: VALIDATION RECORD

Date: 26 October 2026
Baseline: nidhinjs/prompt-master 1.8.2 at 49b3a72469b1e7287d66387004ccfcca90e5968a
Candidate: prompt-master-openai 1.8.2-openai.1

Method

Two separate agents generated responses to the same twelve synthetic prompt-writing requests. Each read its assigned skill and relevant references. Neither executed the generated prompts. A third agent compared the paired responses without source-version labels. A/B order was balanced across cases. Distinctive formatting could still reveal the source style. The available parent model configuration was inherited; an exact service snapshot and runtime settings were not independently recorded.

The review criteria were preserved intent and facts, realistic capabilities, authorisation, usable output, clarification and avoidable friction. The cases were written during development and were not an untouched holdout. This is a single qualitative model review, not a statistical result or independent human evaluation.

Observed result

The judge preferred the candidate in 7 cases, preferred the original in 0 cases, and recorded 5 ties.

The original handled several important cases well, including unavailable browsing, hostile document content, review-only boundaries and corrected handovers. The clearest candidate advantages were delivering an adapted proof prompt without an irrelevant question, preserving existing authorisation while allowing workspace discovery, and supplying a portable extraction schema. One original response was flagged for adding commentary despite an explicit request for no other outputs. These are observations about the generated prompts only.

Packaging checks

The bundled Skill Creator validator passed. Additional checks confirmed local references and anchors, the original nine intent dimensions, templates A to N, diagnostic identifiers 1 to 37, UI metadata, and the unchanged original MIT licence. The research report rendered in a local headless browser at desktop and mobile widths without document overflow; both layouts were inspected. Final archives and the installed copy are checked against source-file hashes.

Limits

No downstream ChatGPT or Codex task-success benchmark was run. No token or monetary savings were measured. The standalone ChatGPT text was inspected for self-containment but did not receive a separate behavioural run. The full skill was tested through its file-based references. Packaging checks do not prove that the active chat has reloaded its skill registry. Existing non-OpenAI profiles are retained as legacy material and have not been independently validated.

Post-comparison changes were limited to template contents links, typography, attribution and packaging. The tested prompt-writing rules were not revised after the comparison.
